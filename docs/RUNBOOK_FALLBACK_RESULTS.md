# Did independent verification close the runbook gap?

**The deterministic fallback completed both healthy observer-outage cases and all six regression trials, using no model tokens. It completed 15 of its 16 planned trials; the final trial was interrupted by an external service restart and remains unassessed. The preregistered promotion gate is unmet.** Structured completed 16/16, basic 15/16, and the original runbook 14/16. This is useful development evidence, not a fully passed validation or a production-readiness claim.

The [prospective plan](RUNBOOK_FALLBACK_EXPERIMENT.md) declared 80 trials: eight conditions, five variants, two repetitions. Run `experiment-04cabcfa` started on GCP on 2026-09-24 at 04:44:57 UTC. The controller finalized **79 trials** before its service was stopped at 06:41:27 UTC. Trial 80 had started; its original worker record remains `running`, without a final outcome or audit. [Offline recovery accounting](validation/runbook-fallback-comparison.json) classifies that attempt as interrupted using retained service-stop evidence. All 80 planned attempts are accounted for, with **60 supported completions, three measured completion misses, one unassessed actor trial, and 16 unscored controls**. Nothing was rerun or replaced.

**Dated follow-up, 24 September 2026:** the [service-recovery correction and separately declared validation](SERVICE_RECOVERY_RESULTS.md) address the lifecycle failure. They do not replace trial 80, change this denominator or satisfy this study's original promotion gate. The historical result below remains unchanged.

## Interruption and evidence recovery

The system journal records a stop and restart of `autolab-fallback-cbddb25.service` during `apt-daily-upgrade`, immediately after an unattended package update. This timing is consistent with a maintenance-triggered service restart; the initiating process was not independently authenticated. It occurred about **1 hour 56 minutes 31 seconds** into the study, before its four-hour timeout. Disk and memory exhaustion were not indicated by the inspected host records.

The service used control-group termination. The controller and its detached janitor remained in that same systemd group, so interruption also defeated automatic final accounting and cluster cleanup. The temporary provider credential was removed. The restarted service exited with status 1 before launching a replacement experiment; no generation was retried. The restarted service's exit status is not presented as the interrupted controller's missing exit status.

Original artifacts and host/service logs were archived **before manual cleanup**, without inventing `accounting.json`, `cleanup.json`, `comparison.finished` or a result for trial 80. Archive SHA-256: `f3b64f9268ab4fab5e56c6457c1a6da3e21b51e93cffc1d3d9c446224ebc268a`. The archive is retained privately both locally and on the stopped GCP disk. It contains runtime credentials and private provider content and must not be published wholesale.

The owned-resource janitor was then invoked manually, deleting only this study's cluster. The [cleanup receipt](validation/runbook-fallback-cleanup.json) and [execution receipt](validation/runbook-fallback-comparison-receipt.json) preserve that distinction. The VM is confirmed `TERMINATED`; the installed v0.1.1 release is unchanged.

The [recovery exporter](../scripts/recover_interrupted_report.py) leaves raw files untouched. It checks the ordered trial prefix, contradictory identities, service-stop timing and matching cleanup receipt; it labels the derived accounting and preserves original trial hashes. Worker partial records normally omit repetition, so the report explicitly identifies its ordered-manifest source. Trial 80's outcome, final verification, duration and audit remain unknown. Two recovered exports are byte-identical. [Reproduction instructions](REPRODUCING.md#reproduce-the-interrupted-fallback-comparison) distinguish this process from exporting a controller-finalized run. Hashes establish integrity comparisons, not independent authentication.

## Validation and decision

The experiment runtime was frozen at [`cbddb250`](https://github.com/rvharsha/Autonomy-Lab-/commit/cbddb2503191da285bcfc7c88496ef712ce046a0), with 205 staged source-file hashes checked before launch. Its release identifier is `b36f6923a1da91c23e02ff61064ae2e8a94881d2e0034f9b0e209b3016f9c14d`. Agent code, prompts, budgets, broker and verifier were unchanged. The study froze source and application conditions, not all host operating-system maintenance.

Before execution, [exact-source CI](https://github.com/rvharsha/Autonomy-Lab-/actions/runs/35954932484) passed 752 tests, lint, 17 real acceptance checks and four model-free handoff trials with matching duplicate exports. Four [Fable source reviews](validation/runbook-fallback-reviews.json) completed through direct Anthropic. The offline recovery exporter was added only after the interruption, with separate [Fable review and dispositions](validation/runbook-fallback-recovery-review.json); it changes reporting, not the frozen actors, scoring, budgets or evidence. Fable reviewed source, not the authenticity of these observations or this results interpretation.

All **79 finalized trials** have assessed scoped audits, covering 9,042 audit events. There were no recorded false/unsupported completions, unsafe/stale/duplicate proposals, unmatched successful mutations, missing acknowledged operations, malformed audit lines or protected-state damage in those assessed trials. Trial 80 remains unassessed; this is not an 80/80 clean-audit claim. Audit scope covers Kubernetes mutations by the named broker, observer and verifier identities in the trial namespace, not a hostile host or every external effect.

The comparison is accounted for, but the fallback's **promotion gate remains unmet**: it has seven of eight required combined-failure completions and fifteen of sixteen assessed audits. Do not treat the interruption as actor success, actor failure, or evidence of a structured-state advantage over the fallback. The one observed structured/basic difference also cannot establish a representation-specific causal effect: prompts and internal tools differ, the sample is small, and provider behavior is nondeterministic.

Before another live study, harden the job's termination and recovery boundary against host maintenance, including an independently surviving cleanup mechanism and a real service-stop acceptance test. Any subsequent validation must have a separate declaration and denominator; it must not replace trial 80. The original [48-trial comparison](AGENT_VALUE_RESULTS.md) remains separate. Cross-incident memory and broader repair authority are still [deferred](FINDINGS.md#current-capability-and-explicit-deferrals).
